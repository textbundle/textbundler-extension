TextBundler

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-23

Summary

TextBundler does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The extension has no analytics, no telemetry, no remote servers, and no user accounts. Everything happens locally in your browser.

What the extension does

When you click the toolbar icon or use the context menu on the page you are currently viewing, TextBundler:

  1. Reads the HTML of the active tab to extract the article.
  2. Downloads the images referenced by that article directly from their original hosts, using your browser's normal network stack.
  3. Converts the HTML to Markdown and packages it together with the downloaded images into a .textpack file.
  4. Saves that file via your browser's normal download mechanism.

Nothing is sent to the extension authors. Image requests go directly from your browser to the same servers that hosted the article — exactly as if you had right-clicked and saved each image yourself.

Permissions and why they exist

activeTab and scripting
Inject the extraction script into the page you are viewing, only when you explicitly invoke the extension.
downloads
Save the generated .textpack file to your downloads folder.
contextMenus
Add the Archive Page as TextBundle entry to the right-click menu.
notifications
Show a confirmation when an archive succeeds or fails.
storage
Store your extension preferences (figure and table output format). Synced through your browser's built-in settings sync if you have it enabled; never sent to any third party.
Host permissions
The extension fetches images from the same hosts that served the article you are archiving. It does not run scripts on arbitrary pages in the background; the content script is injected on demand for the single tab you invoke it on.

Data the extension stores

The only data stored by the extension is your preferences (a couple of formatting options). They live in your browser's extension storage. The extension does not store browsing history, page contents, downloaded archives, image data, URLs, or any identifier of any kind.

Third parties

None. The extension communicates with no third-party services. Image downloads go directly to whichever servers the article links to — those requests are governed by those servers' own privacy policies and your browser's settings, not by TextBundler.

Children

TextBundler is a general-purpose archiving tool and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.
